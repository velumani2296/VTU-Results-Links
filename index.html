Results Schedule

VTU announces semester-wise results twice a year. Odd semester results (1, 3, 5, 7) are typically announced between January and March, while even semester results (2, 4, 6, 8) are declared between June and August. Revaluation results are released in the interim for students who have applied.

How to Check Results?

1

Visit the Official Site Navigate to the VTU results website and find the relevant link for your examination.

2

Choose Your Scheme Select either CBCS or NON-CBCS, depending on your academic scheme.

3

Enter Details Input your University Seat Number (USN) and captcha code, then submit to view your results.

↻

How to Check Revaluation Results?

  • 01

    Visit results.vtu.ac.in and select the appropriate RV result link.

  • 02

    Choose CBCS RV or NON-CBCS RV based on your scheme.

  • 03

    Click on the RV link for your course and semester.

  • 04

    Enter your USN and captcha, then submit to view RV results.
